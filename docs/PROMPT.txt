Build a professional single-page website called "Professional Standards & Risk: Core Principles" that mimics McKinsey & Company's design language and covers the content areas of McKinsey's PSR (Professional Standards and Risk) training program. This is a knowledge reference and self-assessment site.
Design Language (strict McKinsey aesthetic):

Color palette: deep navy (#002744) as primary, white (#FFFFFF) as background, light gray (#F5F5F5) for section backgrounds, McKinsey blue (#0050AA) for accents and CTAs, black (#1A1A1A) for body text
Typography: use Inter or IBM Plex Sans from Google Fonts. Headings bold and large. Body text clean and small. Heavy use of white space.
No gradients, no shadows, no rounded corners (or very subtle 2px max). Everything sharp and geometric.
Thin horizontal rules to separate sections
Cards with a left colored border accent (navy or blue) instead of full card shadows
Icons: use Lucide icons only, stroke style, never filled
No animations except subtle fade-in on scroll
Footer with "McKinsey & Company | Professional Standards & Risk" branding

Page Structure:
1. Hero Section

Full width, white background
Top left: small "McKinsey & Company" wordmark in navy, very small
Large headline: "Professional Standards & Risk"
Subtitle: "Annual Certification Program | Core Principles Reference"
A thin navy horizontal rule below the subtitle
A small progress indicator showing "3 of 7 modules completed" with a thin progress bar in navy

2. What Is PSR Section

Light gray background
Left-aligned heading: "About This Program"
Two column layout: left side is a short paragraph explaining PSR is McKinsey's firm-wide mandatory annual compliance and ethics training. Right side has 3 stat cards: "100% Completion Required", "Annual Recertification", "45,000+ Colleagues Globally"
Stat cards are minimal: just a large bold number/text, a thin navy top border, and a label underneath

3. Module Grid Section

White background
Heading: "Core Principles Modules"
7 cards in a responsive grid (3 columns on desktop, 2 on tablet, 1 on mobile)
Each card has: a Lucide icon, module title, 2-3 sentence description, and a status badge ("Completed" in green or "In Progress" in amber)
The 7 modules are:

Anti-Corruption & Anti-Bribery (Shield icon) — Never engaging in bribery or corruption. Interacting properly with government officials. Understanding the FCPA and UK Bribery Act.
Conflicts of Interest (GitMerge icon) — Identifying, disclosing, and managing actual or potential conflicts. Protecting client confidentiality across engagements.
Workplace Conduct & Anti-Harassment (Users icon) — Maintaining an inclusive, respectful, and caring meritocracy. Zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination.
Information Security & Data Privacy (Lock icon) — Keeping confidential information secure during and after employment. Complying with GDPR, CCPA, and firm data handling policies.
Human Rights & Labor Standards (Globe icon) — Upholding UN Global Compact principles. Prohibiting forced labor, child labor, and human trafficking across operations and supply chain.
Trade Controls & Sanctions (AlertTriangle icon) — Abiding by all applicable trade controls, export restrictions, and sanctions regimes. Not engaging with prohibited entities or jurisdictions.
Environmental Sustainability (Leaf icon) — Understanding McKinsey's net-zero commitments. Integrating ESG considerations into daily decisions and client work.


Mark modules 1, 3, and 4 as "Completed" (green badge). The rest show "Pending" (gray badge).

4. Key Obligations Section

Navy (#002744) background, white text
Heading: "Your Core Obligations as a McKinsey Colleague"
Two column checklist layout using Lucide CheckCircle icons
Items:

Never offer, accept, or facilitate bribes or improper payments
Disclose all actual and potential conflicts of interest immediately
Protect all client confidential information at all times
Complete annual PSR certification before the stated deadline
Report suspected misconduct via Got a Concern? immediately
Abide by all applicable trade controls and sanctions
Maintain complete and accurate records at all times
Treat all colleagues with dignity, respect, and inclusion



5. Reporting & Escalation Section

White background
Heading: "How to Raise a Concern"
Three horizontal steps with thin numbered circles:

Identify — Recognize behavior that may violate Our Code, firm policy, or the law
Report — Submit a report via "Got a Concern?", hosted by a confidential third-party provider. Anonymous reporting is available.
Protection — McKinsey prohibits retaliation of any kind for good-faith reporting


Below that: a subtle info box with navy left border: "The Professional Standards Committee (PSC) reviews all cases of alleged misconduct. Consequences range from performance downtick to separation from the firm."

6. Self-Assessment Quiz Section

Light gray background
Heading: "Knowledge Check"
Subheading: "Test your understanding of PSR core principles"
5 multiple choice questions, one at a time, with Next button
Questions:

"A client offers you a luxury watch as thanks for your work on a project. What should you do?" — Options: Accept it graciously / Decline and report it to your manager / Accept if the value is under $100 / Ask a colleague what they would do — Correct: Decline and report
"You realize you have a personal relationship with a senior leader at a company your team is now engaged with. What is your first step?" — Options: Do nothing unless asked / Immediately disclose the conflict to your engagement manager / Recuse yourself without telling anyone / Complete the project and disclose afterward — Correct: Immediately disclose
"Which law governs US companies bribing foreign officials?" — Options: UK Bribery Act / GDPR / FCPA / Sarbanes-Oxley — Correct: FCPA
"How frequently must McKinsey colleagues certify compliance with firm policies and complete PSR training?" — Options: Every 2 years / Once at onboarding / Annually / Every quarter — Correct: Annually
"Where should concerns about Code violations be reported?" — Options: Directly to HR only / Got a Concern? platform / Your local office managing partner / LinkedIn — Correct: Got a Concern?


After all 5: show a results card with score, a "Well done" or "Review recommended" message, and a navy CTA button: "Return to Modules"

7. Certification Footer Banner

Full-width navy bar
Left side: "Annual PSR Certification" | "McKinsey & Company"
Right side: a clean button "Complete Remaining Modules →" in white with navy border
Very small text below: "Completion of all modules is mandatory. Certification deadline applies to all active colleagues globally."
